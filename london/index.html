Common questions

London & North Kent — the basics

Which areas of London and Kent do you cover?

We work across South East London — Greenwich, Lewisham, Bexley, Bromley, Southwark and Lambeth — and the North Kent corridor around our Ebbsfleet base: Dartford, Ebbsfleet, Gravesend, Northfleet, Bexleyheath and Sidcup. We do not cover Central or West London.

Are you the same company as Harry Handyman in Wales?

Yes. Harry Handyman Service has run in South West Wales for years, and the London & North Kent crew is the same business working from a second base at Ebbsfleet. Same flat-rate pricing, same workmanship guarantee, same standards.

How does flat-rate pricing work?

We agree one price for the job before any work starts, so you are never billed by the hour and never surprised at the end. If you want extra jobs handled while we are on site, they are charged at a fixed add-on rate rather than a new call-out.

Is your crew DBS-checked and insured?

Yes. Every member of the crew is DBS-checked and the business is fully insured. That matters most for landlord, HMO and short-let work, where we are often entering an occupied or unattended property on your behalf.

Which service should I choose?

Choose Handyman Services for general repairs, installs and to-do lists in your own home. Choose Landlord Services for compliance, void turnarounds and HMO works. Choose Short-Let & Airbnb for between-guest checks on a rental. Renters’ Rights explains what your landlord must fix if you rent your home.
