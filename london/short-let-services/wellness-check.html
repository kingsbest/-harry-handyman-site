What it is

The Between-Guests Walkthrough You Can't Do From 200 Miles Away

Managing a short-let remotely means trusting that the property is actually guest-ready — that the cleaner turned up, nothing's broken, the heating works, and the last guest didn't leave a problem you'll only hear about from a one-star review. An AirBnB Maintenance Inspection closes that gap: someone physically attends, checks the property top to bottom, and tells you the truth before the next guest arrives.

Every visit ends with a timestamped photo report to your phone, room by room. You see who arrived, when, what they checked, and anything that needs attention — with a fixed-price quote to put it right, or minor fixes done on the spot if you approve.

Works as a one-off before a high-stakes booking, or as a standing arrangement between every changeovers. We coordinate with your existing cleaner and key holder — we're the check on the property, not a replacement for your cleaning.

Maintenance inspections — frequently asked

How quickly can you attend?

Often same-day or next-day across London and North Kent, depending on your postcode and the time you contact us. Message us the address and check-in date and we'll confirm.

Do I need to be there?

No — that's the point. We access the property, check it, and report back. You never have to be on site.

How do you get in?

Key safe, smart lock, or a key handover we arrange in advance. We coordinate with your existing cleaner or key holder so access is sorted before we arrive.

What happens if you find damage?

You get a photo of it and a fixed-price quote to put it right. If it's minor and you approve, we fix it on the visit. Nothing gets done without your say-so.

Do you work alongside my cleaning company?

Yes. We're the independent check on the property, not a replacement for your cleaner — the two work well together.

Can I set this up for every turnover?

Yes — one-off or standing arrangement. Recurring turnover cover is cheaper per visit; ask about a monthly plan.

Are you insured and checked?

Fully insured, and the crew is DBS-checked. Engineer-led standards throughout.

What areas do you cover?

London and North Kent, including Ebbsfleet and the surrounding towns.
