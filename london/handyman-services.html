Handyman services — frequently asked

What areas do you cover in London and North Kent?

We work out of an Ebbsfleet base and cover South East London and the North Kent corridor: Greenwich, Lewisham, Bexley, Bromley, Southwark and Lambeth, plus Dartford, Ebbsfleet, Gravesend, Northfleet, Bexleyheath and Sidcup. We're not covering Central or West London for now. If you're just outside that patch, ask anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we can get to you.

How does your flat-rate pricing work?

Every job on our list has a fixed price. You know what you'll pay before we lift a tool. If your job isn't on the list we quote it, but it's still a flat price agreed up front, not a running meter. No day rates, no hourly billing, no bill that arrives bigger than the number you agreed.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. Send a photo of the job on WhatsApp and you'll usually get a price back the same day.

How does the £12 extra job work — what counts as a small extra job?

Once we're booked in and on site, a small extra job is roughly a ten to fifteen minute task you'd otherwise pay a full callout for: hanging a picture, changing a bulb in a fitting, adjusting a door catch, tightening a loose handle, refitting a toilet seat. Those are from £12 each, added to the same visit. Bigger jobs are quoted separately, but usually at a discount against a standalone callout because we're already there.

Do you supply materials, or do I need to?

Either. We can supply everything, fit items you've bought yourself, or mix the two. Materials we supply are charged at cost plus supply. Tell us which you'd prefer at the quote stage so the price we give you is the price you pay.

What if my job needs a Gas Safe engineer or a Part P electrician?

We don't carry out gas work or notifiable electrical work in-house. We work with Gas Safe registered engineers and Part P registered electricians and coordinate their visit for you. Their fee is shown separately on your quote from our labour, so you can see exactly what you're paying for and to whom.

Are your crew DBS-checked?

Yes. Every crew member is DBS-checked and the certificate is available on request. We're also insured for domestic and commercial work. Ask to see any of it at your visit and we'll bring it with us.

How quickly can you come out?

Same-day for genuine emergencies — no heat, no water, no security. For booked handyman work it's typically within a few days, and we'll tell you the honest availability from the diary when you call rather than promising a slot we can't hold.

Do you work evenings or weekends?

Yes, though Saturday and evening slots book out first, so book ahead where you can. If you need an out-of-hours visit, say so when you enquire and we'll tell you what's actually free.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. Our workmanship is guaranteed — if something we installed or repaired isn't right, we come back and put it right. That's the whole point of being a named crew you can reach directly rather than a subcontractor who's moved on.
