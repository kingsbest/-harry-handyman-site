About us

About Harry Handyman

Quality property maintenance shouldn't come with weeks of waiting. Harry Handyman bridges the gap between the independent tradesman and the large contractor — giving you the best of both.

Kins Harry, founder — the person behind every Harry Handyman job.

The founder

Meet Kins

I'm a chartered engineer, with a BEng and an MEng, and I spend my days consulting on mission-critical systems — the kind where failure simply isn't an option. I've worked with major names in power and industrial engineering, and helped develop products that are on the market today.

So why the toolbox?

I bought an old house and rarely had to worry about it. But friends and family kept calling me round to their brand-new builds — cracks showing up weeks after moving in, carpets that cover the floor but don't protect it, kitchens and balconies in awkward spots, bathrooms that were never sealed properly, sharp cabinet edges in homes full of young children. The same story, home after home: either the builder had missed something, or the buyer had been sold a place that didn't quite fit their life.

It frustrated me that a bit of care and the right eye would have prevented all of it. So I started Harry Handyman — to bring the standard I hold in mission-critical engineering to the homes people actually live in.

The personal touch, scaled up

Led by Kins. Backed by a trusted team.

From custom carpentry and outbuilding restorations to complex multi-trade repairs, no job is too big — and nothing sits on a waitlist for weeks.
